Three personalities, one sunflower

The same three screens drawn three ways: Home, a book page, and the reader with the highlight toolbar open. Colors stay exactly as in v25. What changes is type, shape, density, and how much of the community shows up around each book.

Orange #F5931F Peach tint White base Slate Mauve avatars

Books, people and messages are sample content. One accessibility fix applies in all three: text on orange buttons is dark ink instead of white, because white on #F5931F measures 2.3:1 and fails WCAG AA. Orange text links use a deeper shade (#9A4F00) for the same reason.

A

Warm, faithful, communal

A church-family reading circle. People sit around every book: friends' faces on covers, a shared line of the day, the book room one tap away. A bookish serif carries greetings and quotes; Inter carries everything you tap.

B

Vibrant, proud, African

Lagos-modern energy. Orange fills whole bands at the top of the main screens, sunflower petals become a pattern, and a bold grotesque gives headings presence. Rankings and live rooms feel like events.

Type Bricolage Grotesque + InterShape chunky, 14px cornersOrange full-width hero bandsSocial live activity, rankings
C

Calm, literary, premium

A quiet library. Lots of white, an editorial serif for titles and reading, hairline rules instead of boxes. Orange appears only where you act, which makes every button easy to find. Community stays one tap away instead of on every surface.

Type Newsreader + InterShape crisp, 10px cornersOrange actions onlySocial present but quiet

How to pick

  • Reply with A, B or C, or mix them, for example "C's reader with A's home".
  • Whichever you choose becomes the design system: tokens, type scale, components and states, then all 11 flows as a clickable prototype.
  • All three already fix two v25 problems: the reader header sits below the status bar, and the highlight toolbar appears above the selection, so it never covers the selected text.
