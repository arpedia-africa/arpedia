Credits and plans

Credits are how you unlock books beyond the free preview, message authors and send tips. 1 credit is ₦3.

What a book costs

  • Every book costs ½ credit per page, shown before you start. A 250-page book is 125 credits.
  • Pay upfront for the whole book, or pay as you read, one chapter at a time. Both add up to the same price.
  • Changed your mind? Undo a chapter within 10 minutes and get the credits back.
  • The first 10% of every book is free, and re-reading is always free.

Where credits come from

Your plan gives you credits every period: 300 on Monthly, 800 on Quarterly and 3,000 on Yearly. Unused plan credits carry over one period. Subscribers can top up: 200 credits for ₦600, 500 for ₦1,500 or 1,000 for ₦3,000.

What if my credits are missing?

Store purchases can take a few minutes to arrive. If nothing shows after 30 minutes, open Help and tap "Contact support" with your receipt number.
