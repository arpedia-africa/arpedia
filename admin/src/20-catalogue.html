Description

When the harmattan dust settles over Kano, the narrator sits down to write the letters she never sent. Twelve letters to three daughters, one for every hard season: the loss of a husband, a failed shop, a faith that went quiet and came back. Warm, plain and honest, a book for anyone who has been far from home and still believed in it.
