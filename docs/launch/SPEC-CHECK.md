### Patterns behind many rows
- **Notification queues nobody reads.** `user_notify`, `book_notify` and `author_notify` have no consumer, and `trust_notify` is drained only by a manual route. Most pushes and emails in flows 03, 08, 09, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 are never sent. One consumer per queue in `workers/api` (with cron wiring) closes many rows at once.
- **"Coming soon" toasts in front of screens that exist.** Book page, reader, library and inbox still toast where `/credits`, `/plan`, `/rooms`, `/author/[id]` exist. `origin/flow/17-journeys` fixes part of this; the rest is listed per flow.
- **Store and providers.** No RevenueCat SDK in the app (local test store only), no Flutterwave web checkout, push sender is a mock outside staging and production, Resend and Flutterwave secrets and real Supabase projects are owner items.
- **Sanctions and reports.** `active_sanction` is not checked by comment, DM, room and review RPCs; `create_report` refuses several content types.
- **Analytics.** Most flows fire few or none of their spec's events.
