# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Readers in Nigeria and across Africa, mostly on mid- and low-end Android phones, often on mobile data. Core audience: Christian fiction and clean-romance readers, readers of African stories, church and campus book clubs. They read in short sessions (commute, evening, Sunday afternoon) and want company around a book: friends, clubs, the author.

Authors: emerging Nigerian and African writers who apply in the app, submit manuscripts (EPUB, DOCX, PDF), talk with readers, and earn a fixed ₦1.60 for every credit readers spend on their books, paid monthly.

Staff: admins and publishers who format and review books, moderate, and run payouts from a web console (out of scope for the app prototype).

## Product Purpose
A subscription reading community. Members subscribe, get credits on every renewal, read a free 10% preview of any book, and unlock books with credits, either all at once or chapter by chapter as they read. Every book has a room; readers highlight, discuss paragraphs, join clubs and reading rooms, follow and message authors, tip and gift. Success: readers finish books and come back for the conversation; authors earn and build a following.

## Brand Personality
Vibrant, proud, African. Lagos-modern energy with warmth and faith underneath. Confident, celebratory, never preachy. Copy is plain and friendly, Nigerian-English natural ("Join the room", "Who's reading tonight").

## Anti-references
- Arpedia v25: orange on every element with no hierarchy, bare empty states, repeated identical rails, toolbar overlapping selected text, header under the status bar.
- Generic SaaS cards: same-size icon + heading + text grids, pastel gradients.
- Western stock "cozy bookshop" cream and serif look.
- Afro-kitsch: tribal-print wallpaper everywhere. Pattern is one motif (sunflower petals), used in hero bands only.

## Design Principles
1. The book is the center; people orbit it. Every book screen shows who is reading, the room, the author.
2. Bold where it celebrates, calm where you read. Orange bands and big type on discovery; the reader page stays quiet and legible.
3. Honest money. Prices, credits and what they unlock are stated plainly before any tap that spends.
4. Built for real phones. Works on 360px Android, slow networks, large text, offline.
5. Every state is designed. Empty, loading, error, first-run, and edge cases teach the next step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Dark ink text on orange (white on #F5931F fails at 2.3:1). Orange text uses #9A4F00. Touch targets 44px min. Reader supports font size 14 to 28, line spacing, light/sepia/dark. Respect reduced motion. Do not rely on color alone for highlight meaning or status.
